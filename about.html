About DiniCini

Introduction Of DiniCini

Who We Are

Dini Cini Creations was established in the year 2010, with its roots in the cinema industry. The founder initially began by working in film-related activities, primarily focusing on movie promotions and associated events. During this journey, a pivotal moment brought a significant change in direction. The founder was deeply inspired by the life and contributions of Nalwadi Krishnaraja Wadiyar, especially his unwavering commitment to farmers and his efforts to uplift their lives. His vision, compassion, and dedication towards agricultural development created a strong emotional impact, leading to a profound shift in perspective. Inspired by these ideals, the organization underwent a transformation in its purpose and outlook. The team made a conscious decision to move away from the film industry and instead channel their efforts into meaningful initiatives that reflect the values of Nalwadi Krishnaraja Wadiyar. Since then, Dini Cini Creations has been committed to creating platforms and initiatives that support farmers, raise awareness, and contribute positively to their growth and well-being.

We Respect Farmers

Founded in 2017, We Respect Farmers is a trust-driven social initiative formed by a dedicated team with the vision of creating a direct connection between farmers and the public. The initiative focuses on supporting farmers by helping agricultural products directly reach consumers with fairness, transparency, and community welfare. During the COVID-19 pandemic, the We Respect Farmers team supplied vegetables and essential farm products directly from farmers to the public at affordable prices. The initiative worked without profit motives, maintaining only a minimal amount for transportation and logistics, ensuring maximum benefit reached both farmers and consumers. The movement received strong support from Ravi D. Channanavar, Rachita Ram, and Chandan Shetty, who actively stood with the team during challenging times. In 2026, the trust organized Raitha Santhe (Farmers’ Market), providing farmers an opportunity to showcase and sell their products directly to the public. As part of the initiative, the team donated a storage container worth ₹1.5 lakh free of cost to help farmers safely store vegetables and agricultural produce.
